Deterministic Evidence Classification Rules:

1. Clinical Guideline: Local PDF chunks explicitly ingested into the 'clinical_guidelines' collection.
2. Consensus Report: Local PDF chunks explicitly ingested into the 'consensus_reports' collection.
3. Systematic Review: PubMed articles with 'Systematic Review' in publication_types metadata.
4. Meta-analysis: PubMed articles with 'Meta-Analysis' in publication_types metadata.
5. Randomized Controlled Trial: PubMed articles with 'Randomized Controlled Trial' in publication_types metadata.
6. Observational Study: PubMed articles with 'Cohort Study', 'Case-Control Study', or 'Observational Study' in publication_types metadata.
7. Other / Unclassified: Default classification for evidence where specific study design cannot be verified from reliable metadata.
